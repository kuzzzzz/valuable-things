# 10 Valuable Things

A single-page website exploring **ten valuable things**, why they are valuable, and timelines showing how their value developed over history.

**Live site:** [https://kuzzzzz.github.io/valuable-things/](https://kuzzzzz.github.io/valuable-things/)

## Contents

1. Gold  
2. Diamonds  
3. Crude Oil  
4. Bitcoin  
5. Spices  
6. Fine Art  
7. Fresh Water  
8. Knowledge & Education  
9. Land & Real Estate  
10. Semiconductors

## How to view locally

Just open `index.html` in any modern browser.
