10 Valuable Things

Why they matter to everyday life — and what you can actually do about them.

For the person next to you right now: These systems quietly set the price of food, fuel, data, rent and credit. You do not need to become rich overnight. Understanding the levers and taking small, realistic steps is already power.
Gold Diamonds Oil Bitcoin Spices Fine Art Water Knowledge Land Chips

What you can actually do (starting today)

  • Protect purchasing power: Learn how inflation and currency moves affect your money. Small, regular savings in more stable forms (where legal and affordable) beat hoping prices stay still.
  • Build a skill that pays: Knowledge is the one asset on this list most people can still grow with low capital. Free and low-cost digital programmes exist right now in Nigeria.
  • Watch the real prices that hit you: Fuel, food transport, data and rent. When oil or the naira moves, those costs usually follow within days or weeks.
  • Avoid high-pressure status spending: Diamonds and luxury signals are heavily marketed. Question any purchase that requires months of income for social approval.
  • Start tiny if you invest: Fractional gold, small Bitcoin amounts, or shares in real companies (when offers are open) can be tested with money you can afford to lose or lock away.
  • Use official channels only: For any investment or IPO, stick to regulated platforms and never share PINs, OTPs or recovery phrases.
01

Gold

Why valuable

Scarce, durable, trusted for millennia. Central banks still hold it. When currencies weaken, gold often holds purchasing power better than cash.

Why it matters day-to-day

Inflation and naira moves shrink what your salary buys. Gold prices are one of the signals big money watches; those signals feed into interest rates and the cost of living.

What you can actually do

  • Track the gold price (in USD and naira) so you see the relationship with local inflation.
  • Some Nigerians buy digital or fractional gold via licensed platforms (examples that have been used: Altinvest, Nemo and similar apps). Start only with money you can lock away for years.
  • Physical 1-gram bars exist, but premiums and storage risk are higher for small sizes — research carefully and buy only from reputable dealers with certificates.
  • Never borrow money to buy gold.

Trending & further reading

  • Gold has been near multi-year highs in recent cycles as investors seek hedges against currency and geopolitical risk.
  • Search: “digital gold Nigeria” or “fractional gold investing” for current platform comparisons (always verify regulation and reviews).
  • World Gold Council (gold.org) publishes clear research on gold’s role as a store of value.

How value developed

c. 550 BCE
First pure gold coins — gold becomes formal money.
1971 – now
Dollar no longer tied to gold; gold floats as a crisis and inflation hedge.
02

Diamonds

Why valuable

Beauty + hardness + decades of marketing that linked diamonds to eternal love and social status.

Why it matters day-to-day

Marketing still pushes people to spend months of income on rings and jewellery that often lose value the moment they leave the shop.

What you can actually do

  • Treat diamond purchases as consumption (status or beauty), not as an investment, unless you are an expert in the secondary market.
  • If buying, compare natural vs lab-grown prices and insist on independent grading reports.
  • Question any “must spend X months’ salary” rule — that was invented by advertisers.

Trending & further reading

  • Lab-grown diamonds have put strong pressure on traditional prices and marketing stories.
  • Search for the history of the “A Diamond Is Forever” campaign (N.W. Ayer / De Beers) to see how demand was deliberately created.
03

Crude Oil

Why valuable

High energy density. Powers transport, industry, plastics and fertilisers. Geopolitics and supply shocks move prices worldwide.

Why it matters day-to-day

When oil rises, fuel, generator diesel, food transport and many imported goods usually become more expensive within days or weeks. In Nigeria this shows up quickly at the pump and in market prices.

What you can actually do

  • Watch pump prices and global crude headlines — they explain a large part of sudden cost-of-living jumps.
  • Current opportunity (as of Sept 2026): Dangote Petroleum Refinery public offer is open to Nigerians. Minimum subscription reported around ₦5,250 (10 shares at ₦525). Available via apps such as i-invest and some Moniepoint PoS channels. Offer closes 13 October 2026 — verify official channels and risk before any money moves.
  • Understand that owning shares in a refinery is still equity risk: prices can fall as well as rise.

Trending & further reading

  • Nigerian pump prices have been under pressure again in 2026 amid global oil moves and the post-subsidy environment.
  • Follow reputable local business media for fuel-price and refinery updates. Official IPO details should come only from approved platforms and the prospectus.
04

Bitcoin

Why valuable

Fixed supply of 21 million. Decentralised. Treated by many as “digital gold,” though far more volatile.

Why it matters day-to-day

Extremely volatile. People have made and lost large sums. For someone who needs cash for rent or food this week it is usually a gamble, not a solution.

What you can actually do

  • Only use money you can afford to lose completely.
  • Buy small fractional amounts on reputable exchanges if you want to learn how the market feels — then withdraw to a wallet you control if you decide to hold long-term.
  • Never share seed phrases or recovery words. Never send crypto to “support” or “recovery” agents.
  • Treat most social-media “guaranteed profit” schemes as scams.

Trending & further reading

  • Bitcoin has seen multiple cycles of large rises and large crashes. Institutional products (ETFs in some countries) have increased mainstream access and also volatility at times.
  • Learn self-custody basics before holding meaningful amounts. Start with educational material from established sources rather than influencers.
05

Spices

Why valuable (historically)

Scarcity + long trade routes + status. At times worth more than their weight in gold relative to wages.

Why it matters day-to-day

Classic lesson: when a few players control a scarce desirable good, ordinary people pay high prices. The same pattern appears today in fuel, FX, data and some medicines.

What you can actually do

  • Use the history as a mental model: ask “who controls supply and who sets the story?” whenever prices feel unfair.
  • For small trade, understand local vs imported costs and seasonality rather than chasing “get rich quick” commodity stories.

Trending & further reading

  • Search “history of the spice trade” or “De Beers diamond marketing” for parallel stories of how scarcity and narrative create price.
06

Fine Art

Why valuable

Uniqueness, cultural prestige, provenance and competition among the wealthy turn certain works into multi-million-dollar assets.

Why it matters day-to-day

Clear signal of extreme wealth concentration. Status purchases rarely fix cash-flow problems; skills and income usually do.

What you can actually do

  • Enjoy public museums, digital collections and local creative scenes without needing to own blue-chip works.
  • If you create, treat art as a craft and possible income stream, not as a guaranteed investment.

Trending & further reading

  • Contemporary art remains a high-end asset class; auction records continue to make headlines while most artists earn modestly.
07

Fresh Water

Why valuable

Essential for life, farming and industry. Clean, reliable supply is already scarce in many places and under climate pressure.

Why it matters day-to-day

Water costs (tanker, pure water, farm input) feed directly into household and food prices.

What you can actually do

  • Track and reduce household water waste where possible.
  • Support or look for community and small-business solutions (boreholes, efficient storage, local purification) that lower ongoing costs.

Trending & further reading

  • Climate and urban growth stories increasingly treat freshwater as a strategic resource. Local reporting on water access is usually more useful than global abstractions.
08

Knowledge & Skills

Why valuable

Compounds. Raises productivity and options. Scarce, practical skills still command the best pay.

Why it matters day-to-day

This is the one item most people can still improve without waiting for the system to change. A useful skill is often the fastest realistic path from “I need money this week” to more options next month.

What you can actually do

  • Free / low-cost programmes in Nigeria (check current windows):
    • Federal Digital Training Academy (Coursera licences) — digital skills, AI, cybersecurity, cloud, fintech etc.
    • 3MTT (Three Million Technical Talent) and related tracks.
    • CcHUB GATEWAY-style free gig-skills training (digital marketing, design, video) in various states when open.
    • Google Digital Skills, HubSpot Academy, YouTube playlists for focused practice.
  • Pick one skill that already has paying demand near you (phone repair, sales, content, basic coding, accounting, driving, catering at scale) and practise until you can deliver for money.
  • Build a simple portfolio or proof of work before chasing certificates alone.

Trending & further reading

  • Search “Digital Training Academy Nigeria”, “3MTT apply”, “CcHUB GATEWAY” for current application windows.
  • Practical earning skills (not just theory) remain the highest-ROI learning for most people short of capital.
09

Land & Housing

Why valuable

Finite. Location and legal title create value. Major long-term store of wealth in growing cities.

Why it matters day-to-day

Rent is often the largest monthly expense. When housing costs rise faster than wages, more of every salary disappears into shelter.

What you can actually do

  • Treat rent as a core budget line and plan around it rather than hoping it stays still.
  • Document everything (receipts, agreements). Informal arrangements are a common source of loss.
  • Small, patient steps toward secure tenure (even a modest plot or cooperative scheme) matter more than stretching for prestige locations you cannot sustain.

Trending & further reading

  • Urban land and rent remain central cost-of-living pressures in Nigerian cities. Local property and cooperative news is more useful than global real-estate headlines.
10

Semiconductors (Chips)

Why valuable

Physical foundation of phones, banking apps, the internet and AI. Extremely complex and expensive at the leading edge.

Why it matters day-to-day

Your phone, mobile-money apps, POS machines and credit-scoring systems all run on chips. Digital skills that use these tools are one practical way to plug into the value without owning a factory.

What you can actually do

  • Treat your phone and data as production tools, not only consumption.
  • Learn skills that sit on top of the digital stack: mobile money operations, online sales, content, basic coding, customer support, digital marketing.
  • Follow free digital-skills programmes (see Knowledge section) that open doors to remote or local paid work.

Trending & further reading

  • AI and geopolitics have made advanced chips a strategic national asset. For most individuals the practical route is skills that use the technology, not manufacturing it.
